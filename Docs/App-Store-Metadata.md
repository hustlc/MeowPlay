# US App Store metadata draft

## Name

MeowPlay: Cat Sounds

## Subtitle

Play, watch, and connect

## Promotional text

Explore gentle cat sounds, save your favorites, and keep a private journal of how your cat responds—all without an account or microphone access.

## Description

MeowPlay is a playful soundboard made for curious cats and the people who love them.

Choose a friendly intent such as “Come Here,” “I’m Mom,” or “Play With Me,” play a gentle cat sound once, and observe your cat’s response. Save favorite sounds, organize Premium playlists, and record simple reactions in a private journal stored on your device.

FREE FEATURES

- Six free launch sounds across the current catalog
- Up to three favorites
- Your seven most recent plays
- Simple cat-response notes
- No account, ads, microphone, or tracking

MEOWPLAY PREMIUM

- All current sounds, plus future sound packs
- Unlimited favorites
- Custom playlists
- Full play history and response insights
- Access to future sound packs

Subscriptions are available monthly or annually. Eligible customers may receive a seven-day annual trial. The App Store always shows the exact localized price and terms before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in App Store subscription settings.

MeowPlay is designed for entertainment and enrichment. It does not translate animal language or provide veterinary or behavioral advice. Cats may respond differently. Begin at low volume and stop if your cat seems uncomfortable.

## Review notes

MeowPlay is an entertainment soundboard, not an animal-language translator. The English card titles describe playful human intents; they do not claim literal semantic meaning.

Six cards are free in the current launch catalog. To reach the Premium paywall, open any card marked with a lock or tap “See Premium options” in Settings. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are both under Settings > Premium.

No login is required. The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or tracking permission. Favorites, playlists, playback history, and reaction entries remain on device.
