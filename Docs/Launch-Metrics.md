# First 30-day launch scorecard

Use App Store Connect data only for the first release; the app contains no third-party analytics SDK.

| Metric | Validation target | Decision use |
|---|---:|---|
| Product-page conversion | 20% or higher | Rework screenshots/subtitle first if low |
| Install-to-trial | 5% or higher | Revisit free value and paywall entry points if low |
| Trial-to-paid | 30% or higher | Revisit annual offer and expectation setting if low |
| Day-7 retention | 10% or higher | Improve sound quality and reaction journal if low |
| Refund rate | Below 5% | Audit subscription clarity and content quality if high |
| Crash-free sessions | 99.5% or higher | Pause acquisition and fix stability if low |

Do not add dog mode, recording, or AI during this validation window. If conversion is healthy but retention is weak, prioritize the first six-to-eight-sound content update. If retention is healthy but trial starts are weak, test store creative and Premium presentation before expanding scope.
