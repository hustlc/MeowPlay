Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Summary

MeowPlay does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or use your in-app activity for tracking. Favorites, playlists, playback history, and cat reactions remain in local storage on your device.

Permissions

The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or advertising-tracking permission.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and related transaction information under Apple's privacy terms. MeowPlay reads verified StoreKit subscription status only to determine whether Premium features are available.

Support

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you voluntarily include in your message and uses it only to answer the request. Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support and legal obligations.

Children

MeowPlay is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Contact details are provided on the Support page.
